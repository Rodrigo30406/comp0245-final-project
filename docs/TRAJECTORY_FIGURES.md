Base-path figures show the first 2 s above and the full 40 s below, with equal
spatial scales and arrows indicating heading. Error curves include both horizons.
Commands use PRE-step decision times. Extreme excursions indicate simulated
divergence, not realistic travel.

Heading figures show unwrapped yaw and signed angular difference from the expert;
unwrapping may miss turns during rapid divergence between sparse samples.
Saturation figures show raw-command-to-limit ratios and the first recorded clip.
See [saturation and orientation](SATURATION_AND_ORIENTATION.md) for definitions
and sampling limitations. This export contains 66 PNG and 53 PDF files.

Historical exports under `trajectory_validation_20261025` and
`saturation_orientation_verified_20261025` remain unchanged as provenance.
